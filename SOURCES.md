## Market-data figures — sources

All figures on `market-data.html` and in the articles were gathered November 6, 2026.

- **U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026** (published February 2026),
  Rare Earths chapter — primary source for world mine production (390,000 t REO in 2025;
  China 270,000 t; United States 51,000 t; Australia 29,000 t; Burma 22,000 t), world
  reserves (>75m t; China 44m t), U.S. net import reliance (67% in 2025 vs 53% in 2024),
  annual average NdPr oxide price ($69/kg in 2025 vs $55/kg in 2024), and the export-control
  summary (April 2025 controls in force; October 2025 package suspended for one year).
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf
- **International Energy Agency, Rare Earth Elements 2025** (May 21, 2025) — total
  rare-earth demand 78 kt (2021), 91 kt (2024), 123 kt (2030, STEPS), 150 kt (2040);
  cleantech demand 11 kt / 19 kt / 38 kt; top-three refining share 98% / 97% / 92%;
  China mined 60%, refined 91%, made 94% of sintered NdFeB magnets (2024 data).
  https://www.iea.org/reports/rare-earth-elements-2025
- **International Energy Agency, Global EV Outlook 2026** (May 20, 2026) — 2025 global EV
  sales 20.7m, +20% YoY, ~25% of car sales; China ~60% of sales; 2026 forecast ~23m.
- **Shanghai Metals Market, Rare Earth Weekly Wrap-Up** (November 2, 2026) — Pr-Nd oxide
  FOB $115–121/kg (down $3); neodymium oxide FOB $138–140/kg (down $3); terbium oxide FOB
  $1,150–1,238/kg (down $6); Pr-Nd alloy FOB $130–144/kg.
  https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104190051-smm-rare-earth-weekly-wrap-up-light-rare-earth-prices-broadly-fell-while-the-ex-china-industry-chain-continued-to-expand
- **MP Materials, Q3 2026 results** (reported November 5, 2026) — revenue $147.7m (+176% YoY);
  NdPr production 1,018 MT (+41%); NdPr sales 1,020 MT (+94%); materials segment $133.5m
  (+322%); magnetics $18.6m (−15%); $31.6m heavy rare-earth concentrate sale to a U.S.
  aerospace/defense customer; realized NdPr price ~$100/kg; REO production 12,887 MT (−3%);
  GM magnet qualification complete, saleable deliveries expected Q4 2026; commercial Dy/Tb
  separation begins Q4 2026; NdPr run rate >5,000 MT/yr expected for full-year 2027.
  Call transcript: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/06/mp-materials-mp-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/
- **Lynas Rare Earths, FY26 results** (year ended June 30, 2026; ASX, August 26, 2026) —
  revenue A$977.9m (+76% vs A$556.5m FY25); NPAT A$222.4m vs A$8.0m; REO production
  13,089 t; NdPr production 7,260 t; NdPr sales 7,337 t; Q4 FY26 revenue A$288.9m;
  record average selling price A$98.2/kg in the June 2026 quarter (+63% YoY).
  https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260826/pdf/0737tbnynjls6y.pdf
- **MP Materials / U.S. Department of Defense partnership** (July 10, 2025) — $400m
  convertible preferred stock; $110/kg NdPr price floor for 10 years (contract for
  difference); 10-year offtake for 100% of the planned "10X" magnet facility output
  (7,000 MT/yr); DoD ~15% stake on conversion/exercise; $150m DoD loan for heavy
  rare-earth separation; $1bn JPMorgan/Goldman construction financing commitment.
  Analysis: https://payneinstitute.mines.edu/explainer-on-the-mp-materials-department-of-defense-partnership/
- **Apple / MP Materials** (July 15, 2025) — $500m long-term agreement for magnets made in
  the U.S. from 100% recycled materials; $200m Apple prepayment; shipments from 2027.
  https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-and-apple-announce-500-million-partnership-to-produce-recycled-rare-earth-magnets-in-the-united-states
- **General Motors / MP Materials** — binding agreement December 2021; long-term supply
  agreement April 2022; $150m GM prepayments ($100m in 2024 + $50m April 2025);
  Independence facility capacity ~1,000 t/yr NdFeB initially, expanding to 3,000 MT/yr.
- **China export controls** — MOFCOM Announcement No. 18, April 4, 2025 (licences on Sm, Gd,
  Tb, Dy, Lu, Sc, Y; in force, never suspended); October 9, 2025 package (5 more elements
  + equipment/technology controls + 0.1% foreign-content rule; suspended November 7, 2025
  to November 10, 2026); extension into January 2027 announced by Treasury Secretary
  Scott Bessent, September 24, 2026; MOFCOM confirmed September 28, 2026 that the wider
  U.S. trade arrangements run to January 10, 2027 — that statement did not name rare
  earths, and no numbered MOFCOM notice changing the November 10 date had been published
  as of mid-October 2026 (Radial Magnets, November 3, 2026).
- **Lynas / Japan Australia Rare Earths** (March 10, 2026) — 12-year agreement, firm offtake
  5,000 tpa NdPr at a US$110/kg floor. Cited via a secondary summary (Farmonaut) of the
  Lynas announcement; treat as secondary-sourced.
- **Royalty/streaming model** — Franco-Nevada (founded 1986 by Seymour Schulich and Pierre
  Lassonde; market capitalisation ~$35bn, September 2026, per market summaries); Wheaton
  Precious Metals Q3 2025 revenue $476m (+55% YoY); Royal Gold H1 2026 revenue $919.7m
  and $4.15bn acquisition of Sandstorm Gold and Horizon Copper (October 2025); NSR royalties
  typically 1–5% of net smelter return; streams commonly buy at ~20–25% of spot.
  https://www.gold-eagle.com/article/are-these-bankers-better-wall-street
- **Ex-China price gap** — Fastmarkets assessments, August 27, 2026, as reported by
  Discovery Alert (November 2026): dysprosium oxide $270–$330/kg FOB China vs
  $1,250–$1,675/kg Rotterdam (~5x); terbium oxide $1,180–$1,270/kg vs $4,200–$5,000/kg
  (~4x); samarium oxide indicative quotes $11–$13/kg vs $3,000–$4,800/kg (thin markets —
  signal of dislocation, not a clearing price).
  https://discoveryalert.com/analysis/rare-earth-price-gap-ex-china-supply-risk-november-2026/
- **Magnet intensity** — direct-drive offshore wind turbines use up to ~600–650 kg NdFeB
  per MW; semi-direct-drive ~180–200 kg/MW; EVs use ~2–4 kg NdFeB per vehicle (Adamas
  Intelligence via secondary sources). Used as approximate, labelled as such.
- **IEA $6.5 trillion warning** (November 1, 2026) — cited via East Asia Brief (secondary);
  described as an IEA warning, not as a primary IEA publication.
- **Radial Magnets, November 2026 Magnet Industry Update** (November 3, 2026) — China
  domestic prices October 15, 2026 (Pr-Nd oxide 743,000–745,000 yuan/tonne; dysprosium
  oxide 1.40–1.42m yuan/tonne; terbium oxide 6.36–6.40m yuan/tonne); Chinese magnet
  shipments to the U.S. 512 t in August 2026 (−20% MoM, −13% YoY); DFARS 252.225-7052
  sourcing deadline January 1, 2027; G7 June 2026 target of <60% reliance on any single

  https://radialmagnet.com/november-2026-magnet-industry-update/

- **Myanmar (Burma) 2025 mine production:** secondary summaries disagreed (22,000 t vs
  38,000 t). The USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 primary figure of 22,000 t was
  used; the 38,000 t figure was discarded as unverified.

  ~$35bn per September 2026 summaries vs ~$30bn+ elsewhere; Wheaton ~$21bn vs ~$30bn+).
  Only the Franco-Nevada ~$35bn figure is stated, labelled "approximately" and dated.
- **Rare-earth market-size totals:** estimates conflict by definition (ABI Analytics $40bn
  in 2025 → $77bn in 2030 → $118bn in 2035; MarketsandMarkets $5.46bn → $7.39bn;
  Emergen Research $9.82bn → $19.47bn). No single market-size figure is presented as a
  consensus on the site.
- **Canaccord Genuity forecasts** (NdFeB demand ~7.3%/yr to ~365,000 t by 2035; NdPr deficit
  ~7% in 2026; Dy/Tb deficit ~12% by 2035) are forecasts from a secondary summary and were
  not used on the site.
- **MP Materials 2025 full-year revenue ($224m, +10%) and net loss ($85.8m)** were noted in
  research but not used on the site.
