How to read these numbers

Price bases differ. FOB export prices, China domestic prices in yuan, annual average prices, and realized producer prices are different measurements of different things. The table keeps them in separate rows and names the basis in each row; do not compare a row in one section with a row in another as if they were the same price.

Ex-China quotes are indicative. The Rotterdam prices for dysprosium and terbium come from thinly traded markets. They show the direction and scale of the gap, not a clearing price at volume.

Forecasts are forecasts. IEA figures to 2030 are scenario projections (STEPS), not observed data. Market-size estimates from commercial forecasters vary widely by definition and are not shown here as a single consensus figure.

Figures are aggregated public estimates, not investment or professional advice. They are compiled to describe the market this domain serves, not to recommend any investment, purchase, or strategy.
